## **Prompt to Copy & Paste:**

> **Role & Objective:**
> You are a Senior GIS Frontend Engineer working on our "NextStop JGI" college bus tracking system. In our Mapbox GL JS integration, our custom OSRM road-snapped bus routes are visible as dashed red tracks, but selecting a route like "Pg Route" from our bottom dashboard drawer currently shows an incorrect straight blue line and does not isolate the route's specific bus stops.
> Your objective is to wire our bottom drawer route selection directly to our custom OSRM track layers, filter the displayed bus stops, and snap our real-time GPS vehicle tracking directly to the active road track geometry using Turf.js.
> ---
> 
> 
> ### **Phase 1: Route & Stop Isolation on Selection**
> 
> 
> When a user selects a route from the bottom drawer (e.g., clicking `"Pg Route"` with `routeId: 'pg_route'`):
> 1. **Hide Unrelated Geometries:**
> * Remove or hide any static/straight point-to-point fallback blue lines immediately.
> * Use `map.setLayoutProperty(layerId, 'visibility', 'none')` on all inactive OSRM track layers and stop marker layers.
> 
> 
> 2. **Activate the Custom OSRM Red Track:**
> * Locate the specific custom Mapbox layer for the selected route (e.g., our OSRM red track layer).
> * Set its visibility to visible: `map.setLayoutProperty('osrm-track-' + selectedRouteId, 'visibility', 'visible')`.
> * Apply an active highlight style to make the track clearly stand out (e.g., changing line-color to a solid vibrant primary color or thickening the stroke to `6px`).
> 
> 
> 3. **Filter Bus Stops:**
> * Filter our bus stops layer so ONLY stops belonging to the selected route appear on the map:
> `map.setFilter('bus-stops-layer', ['==', ['get', 'route_id'], selectedRouteId]);`
> 
> 
> 4. **Camera Auto-Fit:** Smoothly zoom and pan the camera to fit the bounding box of the selected red track using `map.fitBounds(trackBounds, { padding: 80, duration: 1200 })`.
> 
>

> 
> 
> ### **Phase 2: Live Vehicle Snapping to the Track (Turf.js Integration)**
> 
> 
> Raw GPS telemetry can be noisy and drift off the road. When live latitude/longitude telemetry arrives from our backend/websockets:
> 1. **Install/Import Turf.js:** Ensure `@turf/turf` (specifically `turf.nearestPointOnLine` and `turf.point`) is available in the project.
> 2. **Snap Coordinates to Active Track:**
> * Grab the GeoJSON `LineString` geometry of the currently active OSRM red track.
> * Convert the incoming raw GPS GPS coordinate into a Turf point: `const rawPoint = turf.point([lng, lat]);`.
> * Calculate the exact road-snapped coordinate: `const snapped = turf.nearestPointOnLine(activeTrackGeoJSON, rawPoint);`.
> 
> 
> 3. **Update Bus Marker:**
> * Feed the snapped coordinates (`snapped.geometry.coordinates`) into our vehicle marker source: `map.getSource('live-bus-source').setData(...)`. This guarantees the bus icon smoothly glides *strictly along the red track* without ever jumping across buildings.
>
