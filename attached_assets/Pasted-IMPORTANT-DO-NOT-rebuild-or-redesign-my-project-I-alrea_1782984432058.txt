-------------------------------------------------------

• Start Trip
• Pause Trip
• Resume Trip
• End Trip

Use:

navigator.geolocation.watchPosition()

NOT getCurrentPosition().

GPS Configuration:

enableHighAccuracy: true

maximumAge: 0

timeout: 10000

LOCATION UPDATES
-------------------------------------------------------

Continuously track driver location.

Send updates every 2–3 seconds OR whenever the bus has moved more than 10 meters.

Do not send duplicate coordinates.

Include:

driverId

busId

tripId

latitude

longitude

speed

heading

accuracy

timestamp

Reject GPS points where:

Accuracy >20 meters

Invalid coordinates

Impossible jumps

-------------------------------------------------------

100 meters
