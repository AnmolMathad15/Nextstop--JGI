### Pro-Tips for your Replit Implementation:

* **Layer Ordering**: When adding layers, ensure your `route-layer` is added *below* your labels (like street names) so the map remains readable. You can do this by passing a layer ID (like `road-label`) as the second argument to `map.addLayer(layer, 'road-label')`.
* **Performance**: If you find the map feels sluggish, ensure you are using `map.getSource().setData()` to update the route path rather than deleting and re-creating layers.
